## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-25

First release.

- Nine levels. Levels 1 to 5 use mirrors and beam splitters; levels 6 to 9 add
  converging and diverging lenses and a laser that sends three rays.
- A palette of drawn buttons, pieces that snap to the grid, and undo and redo.
- A runnable ZIP that needs Java 17 or later and no build.
